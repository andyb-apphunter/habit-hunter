Habit Hunter privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

Your habits are stored in your own account so they sync between your devices. Only you can see them. There is no tracking and no advertising.

Where your habits are kept

When you sign in with Google, your habits, ticks, minutes and comments are saved in Google Firebase under your account, with a copy on each device you use. Security rules let only your signed-in account read or change them.

Backups and feedback

If you make a backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save or send it, such as Google Drive, iCloud or email. Beta feedback is only sent if you choose to share it, using your own messaging or email app.

Analytics

Habit Hunter doesn't use analytics, crash reporting or third-party code that tracks you.

Deleting your data

Delete habits inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything from your phone.

Children

Habit Hunter isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
